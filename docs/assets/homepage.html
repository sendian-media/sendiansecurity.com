// ABOUT US

Sendian Integrated Security Services

Sendian Integrated Security Services (SISS) is a systems integrator delivering end-to-end security, infrastructure, communication, and automation solutions for complex environments.

We design, build, and maintain integrated systems that combine surveillance, access control, networking, data infrastructure, automation, and audio-visual technologies into unified, scalable platforms.

With more than a decade of experience delivering large-scale projects across Qatar, SISS supports clients throughout the full lifecycle of their systems, from design and approvals to implementation, maintenance, and long-term operation.
