## Extracted article body

**Sendian Integrated Security Services (SISS)** is currently executing the installation of a  
comprehensive CCTV surveillance system at Qatar Finland International School,  
supporting the school’s infrastructure and security enhancement program.  
This ongoing project includes the deployment of high-definition IP cameras, centralized  
video recording systems, and supporting ELV network infrastructure across the campus.  
The surveillance system is being implemented in alignment with Qatar’s regulatory  
requirements for educational facilities, ensuring proper monitoring of entrances, academic  
buildings, and shared areas.  
SISS teams are actively managing the installation, system configuration, and coordination  
processes to ensure the surveillance infrastructure meets operational standards and  
supports a secure learning environment for students, staff, and visitors.  
Once completed, the system will provide the school with a reliable campus-wide  
surveillance platform designed for continuous operation, simplified monitoring, and long-  
term maintainability.  
This project reflects SISS’s ongoing role as an **ELV and CCTV systems integrator in Qatar,**  
supporting schools, commercial developments, and institutional facilities with  
professionally deployed surveillance and infrastructure solutions.
